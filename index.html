Who I am

I build things that have to look right and work right.

Shaan Jose Baiju is a recent graduate of Bath Spa University, RAK Campus, with a BSc (Hons) in Creative Computing. The work sits between interface and engineering — pages, product visuals, and Android apps.

On the web, the toolkit is HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Visual systems are shaped in Figma and Canva. Mobile work is built in Android Studio with Kotlin and XML. The aim is simple: a clear layout, a calm interaction, and code that holds up.

Schooling ran through Sharjah and the wider UAE — from early years to higher secondary — before three years of creative computing. Now looking for junior roles where design and development share the same brief.
